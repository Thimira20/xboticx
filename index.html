// 01 — ABOUT

Where robots meet ambition

XBOTIX is organized by EIES, Faculty of Engineering, University of Ruhuna. Teams design, build and program robots, then bring them to the arena to compete head to head.

On competition day

  • Teams from schools and universities across the country gather in Galle.
  • Robots face the arena challenges live in front of judges and spectators.
  • Winners are crowned in both the School and University categories.

Full challenge details are in the rulebooks.

School Category

For school students. Teams of 2–5 members with a guardian and a principal's verification letter.

RulebookRegister

University Category

For undergraduates. Teams of 2–5 members, each with a university registration number.
